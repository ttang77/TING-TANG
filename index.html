Main projects

01

Multidimensional biodiversity–ecosystem functioning

Genetic diversity · Species diversity · Multitrophic interactions · Forest productivity

At BEF-China, I study how multiple dimensions of biodiversity shape forest productivity, survival, and structure. This work shows that genetic and species diversity act through functional diversity and interactions across trophic levels.

Related papers eLife · 2022 ↗ National Science Review · 2025 ↗ PNAS · 2025 ↗ Journal of Ecology · 2022 ↗

02

Within-species variation in diverse forests

Intraspecific variation · Community diversity · Tree growth · Restoration

I investigate how maternal lines within tree species respond differently to community diversity. The importance of within-species variation grows as forests develop, with implications for restoration and forest management.

Related paper New Phytologist · 2025 ↗

03

Tree chemical responses to drought

European beech · Genetic variation · VOCs · Drought stress

Using a European beech common garden, I study how genetic background and drought shape volatile profiles. The results reveal strong plasticity, persistent drought effects, and potential non-invasive indicators of climate stress.

I follow curiosity across boundaries

My journey in ecology has taken me from China to Switzerland, and across questions that connect scales—from genes to ecosystems. Along the way, collaboration has drawn me increasingly across disciplinary boundaries, from the natural sciences toward understanding how people interpret evidence, make decisions, and act in different social contexts.

Beyond research, I’m an enthusiastic swing dancer, a watercolor lover, a hiker, and an occasional nonfiction writer. I enjoy moving between different ways of observing the world—through science, conversations, movement, landscapes, and stories.
